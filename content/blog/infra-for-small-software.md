Over the past couple years, I have written an uncountable number of scripts, workflows and applictions that solve a very small niche. Although they are very helpful in doing what I do, I cannot afford to host them. It's too expensive to justify setting up AWS, GCP or Asure for them. I could rent a VPS and host it on them but then it's too much of a hassle to set everything up. 

Therefore a need for a SaaS like environement that let's you host different applications as and when needed.
